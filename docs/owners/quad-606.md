## What it is
The Quad 606 is a powerful amplifier that drives your loudspeakers. There are two main versions, MK I and MK II. The Quad 707 and 909 are close relatives and use the same DADA kit.

## Write these down
- The serial number.
- The shape of the case: square edges or chamfered (angled) edges. Square is usually MK I and chamfered is MK II. But square cases with serials 19,900 to 21,600 are MK IIs.
- Your preamplifier.
- Whether it hums mechanically from inside the case.

## What a recap does
A recap replaces the ageing capacitors on the amplifier boards and the big ones that store power. On a MK I, DADA also updates a few input parts to match the MK II and replaces the internal wiring.

## Optional extras
- **Lower sensitivity.** Changing one resistor per channel makes the 606 need a stronger signal, which suits many modern preamps.
- **Quieter transformer.** If the 606 hums mechanically, the usual cure is DADA's kit with a different kind of transformer. That is mains wiring and a technician's job.

## Good to know
- The big capacitors hold a lot of energy after you switch off. Only a technician should open it.
- Never connect loudspeakers until the technician's checks are done.
