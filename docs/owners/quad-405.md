## What it is
The Quad 405 is a power amplifier of about 100 watts per channel. It drives your loudspeakers. The later version is called the 405-2.

## Write these down
- The serial number.
- What the front says: 405 or 405-2. From serial 62,500, some amplifiers badged "405" already have 405-2 parts inside.
- Whether the inputs on the back are a round DIN socket or RCA (phono) sockets.
- Anything already done to it.

## What a recap does
A recap replaces the ageing capacitors, the chip at each input and the parts that power it. DADA's kit also replaces the loudspeaker terminals and inputs, and the wiring inside.

## Optional extras
- **Lower sensitivity.** Makes the 405 need a stronger signal, which DADA says also lowers noise and distortion. Leave it as standard if your preamp is happy as it is.

## Good to know
- The big capacitors inside stay charged for a long time after you switch off. Only a technician should open it.
- Early 405s have no protection for your speakers if a fault happens. DADA says some protection should be fitted, and sells a board for it.
- The two sources give slightly different figures for checking it afterwards. Both are listed for your technician.
