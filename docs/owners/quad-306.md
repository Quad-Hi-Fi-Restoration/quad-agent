## What it is
The Quad 306 is a compact power amplifier, about 50 watts per channel. It drives your loudspeakers and works well with Quad preamps such as the 34 and 44.

## Write these down
- The serial number.
- Your preamplifier, and whether the 306 is used together with a 606 to drive one pair of speakers.
- Anything already done to it.

## What a recap does
A recap replaces the ageing capacitors, including the two big ones that store power. DADA's kit also updates a few input parts the way Quad later did on the 606 MK II.

## Optional extras
- **Lower sensitivity.** The 306 is very sensitive as standard. Changing one resistor makes the volume control easier to use with most preamps. Skip it if the 306 is used alongside a 606.

## Good to know
- If the 306 cuts out after 10 to 20 seconds of very loud test tone, that is its protection working. It is normal.
- The output transistors have small pink paint dots. If they have turned purple, the transistors have overheated and a technician should check them.
- New loudspeaker terminals go in the order red, black, black, red.
