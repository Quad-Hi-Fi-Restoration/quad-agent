## What it is
The Quad 44 is a preamplifier made from 1979 to 1989. It chooses your source and sets volume and tone. It replaced the Quad 33. Each input is a small plug-in module, so different 44s can have different inputs.

## Write these down
- The serial number. It decides which of DADA's three kits you need.
- The finish: brown (early) or grey (later).
- Which input modules are fitted, and your turntable cartridge if you use one.
- Any faults: inputs changing on their own, clicks, a chattering relay at switch-on, or a thump at switch-off.

## What a recap does
A recap replaces the ageing capacitors, the chips that do the amplifying and the chips that switch the inputs. It includes Quad's own fix for inputs that change by themselves.

## Optional extras
- **Lower noise on the radio and aux inputs.** Removes resistors that 1970s chips needed and modern ones don't.
- **Quieter at volume zero.** For early models that are not fully silent with the volume turned right down.
- **Relay fix.** Only needed if the relay chatters when you switch on.

## Good to know
- Moving coil and other unusual input modules need a special kit from DADA.
- Many of the 44's capacitors have no + or − marking on the board. Your technician must note each one before taking it out.
- If the inputs jump around by themselves, Quad published a fix. It is part of the recap.
