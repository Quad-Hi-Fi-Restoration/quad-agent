## What it is
The Quad 303 is a transistor power amplifier. It drives your loudspeakers and is usually paired with the Quad 33 preamplifier.

## Write these down
- The serial number. It decides which adjuster part your technician needs.
- Your loudspeakers. Old Quad electrostatic speakers made before serial 16800 need a small change before use with a 303.
- Your preamplifier.
- Anything already done to it, such as an earlier repair.

## What a recap does
The 303's big capacitors dry out with age. DADA says this makes it sound as if it is "behind a curtain", with weaker bass and less punch. A recap replaces the capacitors and the small adjusters. Then a technician sets the amplifier up again with a meter.

## Optional extras
- **Lower sensitivity.** Makes the 303 need a stronger signal, which can suit modern sources. Only worth doing if your preamp can drive it.
- **LED mains light.** Replaces the little neon lamp if it no longer lights.

## Good to know
- Setting up after the recap is done with the amplifier switched on. That is always a job for a technician.
- DADA advises against reworking very old driver boards and offers new replacement boards instead. Your technician can tell which you have.
- The two sources give different figures for the final adjustment. Both are listed for your technician.
