## What it is
The Quad 34 is a preamplifier from the 1980s. It chooses your source and sets volume and tone, and it has a phono stage for turntables.

## Write these down
- The serial number. It decides which parts list applies.
- The finish (brown or grey) and whether the inputs on the back are round DIN sockets or RCA (phono) sockets.
- Your turntable cartridge, if you use one, and whether it is moving magnet or moving coil.
- The fuse rating printed on the back panel.
- Anything already done to it, such as an earlier recap.

## What a recap does
The 34's capacitors and chips age. A recap replaces the capacitors and, with DADA's kit, fits better modern chips. It is the most common job and the best place to start.

## Optional extras
- **Phono stage changes.** There are changes for moving magnet and moving coil cartridges, and a way to turn the phono input into an extra line input if you use a separate phono stage.
- **Output level and balance control fixes.** For when the 34 is too loud for its power amp, or the balance control has become noisy.

## Good to know
- Units at exactly serial 8000 sit between two parts lists. A technician needs to look at the board before parts are ordered.
- On some capacitors the markings on the board are known to be wrong. The guide tells the technician which way round they go.
- Always fit the fuse rating shown on your unit's back panel.
