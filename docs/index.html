PROVENANCE & CARE

Keep the knowledge accountable.

A part value without its source—or a clear note that it is uncertain—is not enough.

A

Sources are named

Technical guidance points to service data, diagrams and restoration references. Conflicts are recorded for review.

Browse sources & credits ↗
B

Authors are credited

Project writing has its own licence, and every manual, diagram and upgrade guide is credited to the people who made it.

Read content licence ↗
C

Safety comes first

Mains equipment can be dangerous. New restorers can use the guide for external identification and planning; internal work and first power-up belong with a qualified technician.

Read safety guidance ↗

COMMUNITY

Help make the record better.

Restorers can contribute a source, report a correction or help verify a component against a real board. Contributions are recorded so others can understand where the information came from.
